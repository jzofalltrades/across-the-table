# Across the Table

**Play it live:** https://jzofalltrades.github.io/across-the-table/

A robot chessboard that plays your online opponent, so it feels like they're sitting across the table. Designed and built by Jayant Bhanse (jzofalltrades).

This is a playable 3D simulation of the machine. You play white: legal squares light green, illegal ones flash red, press the space bar to send your move, and the belt-driven arm with a hook-jaw gripper plays the reply. Press "Show electronics" to see the Raspberry Pi, ESP32, hall sensors and LEDs inside the platform.

On the real board the Pi plays through the Lichess Board API; here a small built-in engine stands in. Built with three.js and chess.js.
